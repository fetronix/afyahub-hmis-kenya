## Notes carried from individual requirements

- **FN-05 Duplicate patient detection** — Still partial: registration-time matching remains heuristic (it warns, it does not decide), there is no master patient index and no merge or split workflow. The portal closes the two-accounts-for-one-person route, not the identity-resolution problem.
- **FN-06 Patient consent capture** — Still partial: consent is captured but does not gate clinical reads. hasActiveConsent() is exposed for that policy decision, which is a clinical-governance call rather than a coding default. The portal adds a disclosure channel that does not consult consent; that is defensible only because the patient is retrieving their own record, and the facility must confirm that reading rather than have it assumed. DHA consent authorisation (OTP) is separately blocked.
- **FN-09 Radiology orders and reports** — Before this audit these routes had no permission check and no facility scope.
- **FN-15 Allergy and interaction checking** — Still partial: there is no drug-drug interaction engine and no cross-reactivity table. The UI says an empty check is not a guarantee of safety rather than implying one.
- **SP-04 Role-based access control** — Still partial for the same reason as before: five pre-existing unguarded routes and the object-level ownership of id-addressed routes outside the portal are unchanged. The portal added guarded surface; it did not fix the legacy surface.
- **SP-05 Tenant and facility isolation** — Still the most serious finding of the audit: the legacy id-addressed routes outside the portal remain unaudited for object-level ownership. The portal is evidence that the pattern can be applied; it is not evidence that it has been applied everywhere.
- **SP-06 Audit trail** — Still partial: the append-only guarantee exists only in an unapplied migration, so a database that has not run 0017 can still have its audit rows edited by a privileged role. Retention and review ownership are also undecided.
- **SP-08 Encryption at rest** — Still an operational control: whole-volume and backup encryption, key custody and rotation remain the hosting provider and facility responsibility. Application-level sealing of two columns does not make the database encrypted at rest.
- **PH-04 MOH 705 routine return** — The previous screen displayed an empty table, a hard-coded period and an export button that downloaded JSON null.
- **IO-08 SDMX / statistical exchange** — Re-open if the certification scope names SDMX explicitly.
