## 1. Executive summary

### 1.1 Overall verdict

**The system is a substantial, persisted HMIS and accounting implementation, not merely a collection of screens. It is not yet ready for unconditional Syrona client acceptance or an unrestricted production go-live.**

There is a real local billing path: clinical activity or itemized invoice → controlled invoice finalization → attributed payment → receipt PDF → explicitly reviewed, source-linked ledger posting → ledger statements and source-to-GL diagnostics. Procurement also extends beyond orders: requisition → approval → PO → GRN/stock receipt → supplier invoice/AP journal → three-way match → supplier payment/journal. These must not be reported as missing just because older audits said they were.

Equally, a working accounting core is not proof that all revenue has been posted, every hospital department is operational, client report layouts are satisfied, or providers are connected. Current source explicitly leaves theatre at scheduling, specialty care at navigation, national returns unavailable, many report entries disabled, and external connectors unverified or sandbox-only.

### 1.2 Major accomplishments confirmed in current source

- Tenant/facility scope resolution, API permission checks, module gates, staff invitations/role restrictions, account/session controls and clinical access logs.
- Patient registration with server-allocated MRNs, bounded/audited directory search, demographics, encounters, vitals, diagnoses, prescriptions, allergy warnings and consent metadata.
- Lab catalogue/specimen selection, collection/accession control, results, verification and explicit investigation completion; reporting-triggered radiology billing; pharmacy FEFO and immutable dispensing-time price evidence.
- Inpatient occupancy history, ward transfer, per-facility bed-night policies, configurable same-day rules, rate evidence and billing recovery.
- Finalized invoice snapshots, explicit patient/insurer responsibility, payment idempotency/attribution, overpayment liabilities, independent refund approval, ledger posting/reversal, financial periods, expenses and financial diagnostics.
- Supplier CRUD, itemized/approved purchase orders, requisitions, GRNs, AP invoices/payments, matching, returns and supplier credits; stock counts and configurable inventory accounting.
- Journal-backed fixed assets, depreciation, transfers, maintenance, disposal, impairment/revaluation and citation-gated tax allowance configuration.
- Dynamic facility PDF letterheads and eight generated document kinds, including A4/thermal receipts. Replacement requires no application deployment. This is no longer a missing feature, but the clinical release controls and historical-document boundaries need correction/qualification (§5, §3K).
- Real operational dashboards, notifications, 19 report engines, CSV/XLSX/PDF exporters, deployment artifacts and backup tooling.

### 1.3 Most important remaining gaps and risks

1. **Latest published CI is red.** Run `43416678522` at `b229c9d` failed the PostgreSQL coverage job and Docker bootstrap. A failure annotation reports `facilities after restore: expected 165 to be 168` at `backup-restore.db.spec.ts:144`. A previous local green run does not override this evidence.
2. **Clinical PDF release checks are weaker than the underlying workflow/UI.** Read-only executable probes of the actual builders generated models for unverified laboratory results, a non-released radiology record with draft findings, and an admission that was not discharged. These are logic reproductions with synthetic mocked persistence, not live-DB or HTTP executions (§5).
3. **Data-exposure remediation remains operationally unclosed.** The repository is currently public. Historical archive paths described in `docs/security/DATA-EXPOSURE.md` are still addressable in GitHub commit metadata. No archive contents were opened in this audit. No evidence of completed containment/account resets/incident sign-off was supplied.
4. **MFA is not implemented end-to-end.** Required-MFA accounts correctly receive no normal session, but have no second-factor completion route. Enabling the flag is not a usable MFA deployment.
5. **Patient attachments and signed-form custody are missing.** Consent metadata/hash and referral-document tables are not a general secure patient-document workflow.
6. **Reports and document navigation are incomplete/inconsistent.** Current catalogue: **144 entries; 23 engine-backed entries, one invoice workflow entry, 120 unavailable entries; 19 unique engines**. Several unavailable printables actually exist in their owning modules. Financial-report PDFs use a separate exporter without facility letterheads.
7. **Budget-versus-actual, department revenue attribution/outputs, receivable ageing, client-approved financial layouts and official MOH/KHIS returns remain incomplete or missing.** Posted-ledger statements are not an assurance that the source ledger is complete.
8. **Stores are labels, not balance-holding locations.** Transfers record movement legs but do not maintain per-store holdings. General adjustments are not department/ward/dental consumption custody.
9. **Theatre clinical execution, dental/other specialty documentation and associated consumable/billing chains are missing.** Scheduling is real; operative workflow is not.
10. **No verified production financial/insurer integration or client UAT.** Britam direct is explicitly unsupported pending documentation. SHA/AfyaLink and M-Pesa code/mocks are not provider acceptance. Hosting, off-host backup custody, restoration on the deployment host and monitoring escalation lack operational proof.

### 1.4 Recommended next step

Authorize the bounded P0 work packages in §11, beginning with incident-response ownership, reproducible green CI/backup verification and clinical-document release enforcement. In parallel obtain the actual Syrona workbooks, role matrix, stationery and financial policies. Then run role-based synthetic UAT with named client signatories. **Do not start another broad rewrite or describe this audit as client acceptance.**

## 7. Independent financial and accounting assessment

### 7.1 Actual usable local chain

**Yes, an implemented invoice→payment→receipt→ledger workflow exists, with an explicit manual/reviewed ledger step.** It is not just an invoice creation form. Current evidence supports implementation and historical synthetic test execution, not a newly repeated live-DB/browser acceptance in this audit.

1. **Define facility chargeable products/services.** Master catalogue adoption does not itself create an approved tariff. Facility service/tariff/effective-date validation controls clinical price resolution. A missing/ambiguous/changed price creates recoverable billing work instead of inventing a fee.
2. **Record performed activity.** Consultation completion, laboratory investigation completion, reported imaging, actual dispensing and billable occupied bed nights carry distinct evidence. A request/booking alone must not be revenue. Pharmacy uses `resolvePharmacyInventoryCharge`, which demands a persisted dispensing, prescription/encounter scope, immutable item/price/date evidence and quantity consistency. Service tariffs are deliberately not medicine prices.
3. **Generate invoice lines.** Server-resolved source identity and cent arithmetic govern amounts. Clinical source transactions stage billing outbox evidence so a response/charge failure is visible and retryable rather than silently lost. Confirm source-completeness and retry policy in UAT.
4. **Finalize.** `finalized-invoice.service.ts:finalizeInvoice` validates recorded data/attributions/responsibility split and writes a snapshot; migration controls freeze financial rows. It records active letterhead ID. A finalized number is not synonymous with paid or posted.
5. **Take payment.** `payments.service.ts` validates amounts/reference, serializes financial changes, checks source-ledger consistency and enforces explicit responsibility attribution and retry identity for finalized invoices. Over-collection requires confirmation and remains credit/liability rather than becoming fictitious revenue.
6. **Issue receipt.** RefundsPanel exposes A4 and thermal through `documentClient.receipt`; builder reads actual payment amount/method/reference/cashier. This path is now present. Verify printer behavior, historical demographics and audit parity.
7. **Post invoice and receipt to GL.** BillingJournalPostingPanel→`postBillingSourceScoped` requires accountant-selected active in-facility accounts, entry date, description and idempotency identity. Invoice posts patient/insurer receivables against revenue. Receipt reduces posted AR and puts excess into selected liability; invoice posting must precede receipt posting. Payment capture does not itself prove this reviewed posting occurred.
8. **Reconcile.** `billing-gl-reconciliation.service.ts` and source-ledger coverage compare independent source families/amounts/dates. They surface posting backlog/missing or inconsistent bridges instead of asserting that equal aggregate totals prove reconciliation. A day's payment on an old invoice must be selected by its own activity date.

### 7.2 Refunds, credit notes and corrections

Refund requests have approval/segregation controls, recorded manual confirmation and links to original financial evidence. The generated “Credit Note” is explicitly a printed refund (`billing_refunds`), not a complete line-level invoice credit/waiver product. Cash refund, AR credit, revenue reversal and insurer adjustment have different economic meanings. Confirm Syrona's intended documents and posting rules before claiming full credit-note accounting. Paid invoice voiding is controlled; corrections should be audit-preserving, not edits to frozen financial history.

### 7.3 Procurement/AP and inventory accounting

Earlier claims of no AP are obsolete. ReceivingPanel invokes `createSupplierInvoice` against a posted GRN; it records subtotal/tax/total, journal link and an immediate three-way match. `paySupplierInvoice` locks invoice, checks prior retry identity, requires acceptable match/variance decision, checks balance and selected facility bank account, writes supplier payment and balanced AP-to-settlement journal. These are **recorded supplier payments**, not executed bank transfers.

Inventory accounting is policy-driven: movement allocations/costs/journal bridges and reconciliation exist. Opening stock, receipt costs, recoverable-tax/expense vs perpetual inventory and supplier returns/credits must be configured and reconciled. Both AP expense recognition and stock capitalization must not count the same purchase twice. Asset acquisition is journal-backed but not an automatic reviewed PO/GRN capitalization handoff.

### 7.4 Financial statements: what totals actually mean

- **Trial view:** posted account debits/credits and net movements over selected interval; formal opening/closing trial balance presentation needs client agreement. Equal debit/credit totals prove balancing, not completeness or correct classification.
- **Income/P&L:** posted revenue minus posted expenses. It excludes any genuine operational event not posted and cannot be advertised as complete hospital profit.
- **Balance sheet:** inception-to-as-of asset/liability/equity balances including accumulated revenue/expense result. It depends on correct opening balances, classified accounts, complete journals and period policy.
- **Receipts/cash:** not revenue. Insurer remittance advice is not cash. M-Pesa status/configuration is not bank settlement. Supplier payment records do not prove a bank moved money.
- **Department revenue:** does not follow merely from a department label on a service or a journal column. The billing posting helper currently sets `serviceLine: null`; confirm/implement actual dimension provenance and refund/cost rules before producing department margins.
- **Budgets:** no functioning budget workflow/model found. Do not derive “budget” from historical income or an assumed percentage.
- **Bank reconciliation:** substantial backend exists; no full staff UX, no live feed proof, and advanced partial/many-to-one matching remains absent.

### 7.5 Finance acceptance pack to require

A synthetic client-approved fixture should include consultation + multi-result lab + imaging + two partial medicine dispensings + cross-midnight bed transfer; patient/insurer split; partial payment/overpayment; denied refund/self-approval; approved refund; reviewed invoice/payment/refund GL postings; PO/partial GRN/AP/match/supplier payment; inventory and fixed-asset cost/depreciation; closed-period refusals; source-to-GL and stock-to-GL tie-outs; approved statement/export totals. Present both unposted backlog and reconciled amounts. The client finance lead must approve chart, opening balances, tariffs, accounts, periods, credit/waiver semantics, same-day bed policy and financial layouts.
