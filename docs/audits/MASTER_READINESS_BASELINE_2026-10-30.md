## Executive decision

**Not ready for an actual facility deployment.** This is an engineering baseline and risk triage, not an exhaustive source audit, clinical acceptance, security certification, facility tariff audit or evidence of deployment. The repository contains substantial existing functionality, and some focused workflows have been exercised with synthetic PostgreSQL data. Major patient journeys, controlled financial reversals, automatic general-ledger reconciliation, facility-specific tariffs, external provider contracts, clinical approval, organisational evidence, recovery and production configuration remain incomplete or unverified. Do not use the test outcomes here as evidence that a healthcare facility is safe to operate on this build.

The review inventoried department components, route and schema modules, migration and CI infrastructure, existing test files, payment/payer adapters, accounting and the billing call sites. It traced the changed inpatient, invoice, payment, laboratory and pharmacy paths in more detail. Other areas are classified conservatively: a route, UI or prior report is evidence of a code artifact, **not** evidence that every authorization, persistence, workflow and integration path works. Existing documents may describe earlier versions; use source and rerun tests before relying on them.

**Environment:** Tests in this increment use only a newly created, isolated PostgreSQL 16.2 database with synthetic fixtures. The historical chain through migration 0032 was applied there under the previously granted synthetic-database-only permission; no existing or production database was accessed or changed. Prior Theatre work and its separate synthetic tests are described in `docs/audits/THEATRE_IMPLEMENTATION.md`. Test results for this increment are recorded separately in the engineering change report after the final checks; nothing here asserts a live provider or clinical test.

The engine's event idempotency key helps prevent a repeated **same source** from producing a second line. It does not prove that duplicate clinical actions have the same source, that all parallel charge operations are serialized, or that voids and cancellations reverse charges. `chargeAndRecord` can receive the ordinary database executor, while invoice charging and recalculation are separate statements; the charge-versus-void/payment race remains **unresolved**. Do not claim final invoice consistency until a common transactional lock protocol and regression tests cover every writer.

`docs/billing/facility-tariff-intake-template.csv` is a **blank intake worksheet**, not an implemented HMIS importer. It has no example prices, facility IDs or codes. The repository's current tariff API accepts individual validated entries, and no CSV preview/import process was established by this review. Before any future import: validate facility ownership and administrator access, duplicate codes/links, unit and effective dates, currency, the actual service mapping and payer applicability; preview differences, obtain facility approval and audit each change. Fields such as payer applicability and currency are not currently proven to be persisted by the tariff schema. Do not silently overwrite existing tariffs.

## Financial and operational controls still required

1. Implement a transaction-safe common protocol for charge creation, total recalculation, payments and voids, including idempotency and recovery on partial failure. Do not delete existing invoices or lines.
2. Implement approved receipt reversal, refund request/approval/execution, adjustment and credit workflows with complete immutable history and period checks. No current action in this increment represents a refund or a provider-confirmed payment.
3. Configure facility-specific chart-of-accounts mappings, then build balanced, idempotent source-linked postings and controlled reversals. Report missing postings, unmatched receipts, remittances and bank lines; an internally balanced trial balance is not operational reconciliation.
4. Complete partial dispensing with a durable idempotency key, prescribed-quantity accounting and return/reversal model. Until then, the changed service only permits a complete quantity equal to the prescription; raw medication status updates return a workflow-required conflict. These deliberate safety restrictions affect existing clients and need a replacement reviewed workflow, not an undocumented workaround.
5. Introduce a verified mapping between prescribed medication, actual item, formulation and strength, and preserve exact batch allocations for recall. Staff selection alone is not clinical product validation.
6. Establish laboratory state transitions, accession uniqueness, specimen and result release/amendment controls, critical-result notification, audit and clinical policy. The focused verification race test does not prove these functions.
7. Replace any full database-error logging that can include SQL parameters or personal data, and make audit/charge failures visible. The shared historical `logAuditEvent` suppresses write errors; the new admission, invoice void, receipt and dispensation writes use transaction-local minimal audit records instead. Other audit paths require review.
8. Audit missing-number semantics throughout the remaining billing/inventory code. `billing.service.ts` has a null-to-zero `toCents` helper, and batch cost/valuation can use zero when cost is missing; do not rely on those as reconciled financial values. Stock receipt in this increment refuses unknown or negative stock, but batch receipt audit is not yet established.
9. Retire or properly authorize and audit the retained unscoped legacy `recordPayment` helper before any new use. The scoped primary payment route has the transaction-local audit; the legacy helper has no actor with which to make one.

## Provider, notification, compliance and commercial blockers

- **SHA / AfyaLink:** separate the official interfaces, obtain facility onboarding and the approved sandbox contract/credentials. Implement and test each authorized capability and callback verification; no production claim is authorized by this work.
- **Britam / PACIS:** obtain the facility/provider contracts and documented APIs before implementing connections. Existing manual paths are not a live API.
- **Daraja / cards / SMS / email:** select and authorize providers, isolate secrets, confirm callback and delivery semantics, and run authorized sandbox tests. Nothing in this increment sends a live message or establishes payment settlement.
- **MOH / FHIR:** obtain the applicable official reporting/IG version, validate mappings and resource references with an authorized validator and facility source data; do not invent codes or claim certification.
- **DPIA / penetration testing / clinical acceptance:** management, legal, security and qualified clinicians must complete their respective reviews; no fabricated approvals or signatures.
- **Commercial readiness:** complete facility configuration, department-specific manuals based on verified behavior, approved tariff import, training, support arrangements, recovery drill and signed facility acceptance report.

## Production migration and reconciliation gate — 0032

Migration `0032_inpatient_active_admission_guards.sql` is additive: it adds partial unique indexes for an active bed and active facility/patient admission. It refuses pre-existing duplicates and same-named unjournaled indexes; it does **not** repair, delete or reassign records. Index construction can block writes and must be planned for the data volume. Production has not been inspected or migrated.

Before any separately authorized deployment: verify a restorable backup in a separate environment; obtain a maintenance window and clinical/operations approval; check migration history and index definitions; produce **aggregate-only** counts for duplicate active bed and patient admissions, mismatched ward/bed/current-patient states and missing/unknown statuses; have authorized staff review and reconcile individual records without deleting history; repeat the read-only checks; then follow the repository migration/preflight procedure and verify the result. Do not use the synthetic test fixtures as a production reconciliation script. A failed preflight is a stop, not permission to alter data.
