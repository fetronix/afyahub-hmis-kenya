## Detailed ordered outstanding work

### Priority 1 — clinical integrity, tariffs, money and ledger

**Stop-ship, engineering/clinical owner:**

1. Agree laboratory collection/result/release/amendment states and accession policy with laboratory leadership. Implement atomic transition validation, collision-safe facility-approved accessioning, audits, cancellation/correction and clinical exception handling; test real race cases with synthetic specimens. Do not infer collection or verification from a screen.
2. Design actual partial dispensing, safe retry identity, documented substitution/product-strength mapping, durable per-lot allocation and controlled returns/reversals. Restore clinically authorized non-dispensing status changes with state guards and audit. Verify roles, allergies and current stock in a clinical acceptance workshop.
3. Complete the common transaction/lock and idempotency protocol across clinical charge creation, invoice recalculation, receipt and void. The current engine selects an invoice, inserts a line and recalculates via separate statements; per-source unique keys alone do not stop charge-versus-void/payment races or concurrent creation of multiple open invoices. Clinical success can precede billing failure.
4. Implement approved receipt reversal, adjustment, refund and patient-credit lifecycle with separate request/approval/execution, immutable source links, period controls and reconciliation. Do not present the existing void function as a refund.
5. Map actual billable events, timings, service units and facility/payer tariffs using facility-authorized catalogues and contracts. Add a secured preview/validation/import path and history; no rates are implied by the blank intake worksheet or synthetic tests.
6. Connect source-linked, balanced, idempotent accounting journals to approved invoice, receipt, payer settlement and refund events. Enforce period locks and controlled reversals. Reconcile operational subledgers, bank statements, remittances and general ledger; `billingReconciliation` remains `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.
7. Finish browser-to-database journeys A–E, including maternity/newborn and clinical theatre work, and produce evidence with permissions, errors, actual audit rows, billing outcomes and facility clinician sign-off. Existing Theatre work is scheduling only.
8. Audit missing-number semantics end to end. The current charge engine's `toCents` and inventory batch cost/valuation paths still contain zero fallbacks; a missing value can be mistaken for zero and must not be used as financial truth until corrected and reconciled. Batch receipt audit and other shared audit-helper users also require review.
