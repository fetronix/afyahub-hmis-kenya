## Evidence categories

- **Verified engineering:** the exact local synthetic checks and paths below passed. A test or screen does not imply the entire module is complete.
- **Engineering outstanding:** implementation, integration, safety or recovery work remains even with perfect configuration. These are not merely waiting for a facility or provider.
- **Facility data/approval:** approved chart of accounts, prices, mappings, operating policies, real migration review and delegated roles are required; the application cannot supply or attest these on the facility's behalf.
- **Provider configuration/verification:** official contracts, isolated credentials, callback controls and expressly authorized sandbox tests are absent. Mocks are not external tests.
- **Human acceptance:** a facility's designated financial, clinical, privacy/security and operational reviewers must perform and sign off their own browser and workflow testing. Automated Chromium is not that acceptance.

## Accounting, source traceability and reconciliation

### What the bridge actually posts

The reviewed bridge in `src/accounting/billing-posting.service.ts` is **explicit and separate** from creating billing events. It does not create a chart of accounts, automatic charges, transfers, payer acceptance or bank evidence. A facility accountant chooses active same-facility accounts and an open-period posting date; exact monetary cents, balanced journals, permission/scope checks, unique source linkage, audit and transactional rollback are enforced. Replays require the same source, retry identity and request hash.

| Source            | Reviewed journal model                                                                                                                         | Preconditions / meaning                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `BILLING_INVOICE` | Debit patient and/or insurer receivables according to the finalized responsibility split; credit selected revenue.                             | Active finalized invoice and matching positive total/split; no conflicting existing line-level journal. This is the accounting classification chosen by the facility, not a payer decision.       |
| `BILLING_PAYMENT` | Debit the selected settlement/clearing asset; credit the attributed patient or insurer receivable, with excess to a selected credit liability. | Active invoice journal and explicit receipt attribution. Partial and full receipts follow the same rules. Recording a receipt or clearing line does not establish an externally settled transfer. |
| `BILLING_REFUND`  | Debit restored receivable and/or the original credit liability, as explicitly allocated; credit selected settlement/clearing.                  | The recorded manual refund and its original receipt must be linked and actively posted; the allocation cannot exceed the original posting. This does not send funds.                              |
| Journal reversal  | An authorized, balanced reversing journal in an open period, with a reason and retry identity.                                                 | Dependent receipt/refund journals must be reversed before their parent. Original records and journals remain. Voiding an invoice with active linked postings is refused.                          |

`accounting_billing_postings` is immutable and links source, journal, amount, allocation, classification and actor. Journal period/balance/immutability guards also exist in migration 0027. Migration **0041 makes all recorded `billing_payments` append-only, including historical unposted receipts**. This deliberately closes a concurrent update race but may block existing administrative cleanup/update paths; review those operational consequences and historical data before any real migration. Do not delete or rewrite financial history to make a migration pass.

The scoped UI and API support source status and identity-only browser recovery. A recorded replay is not a new posting; `NOT_FOUND` does not prove a delayed request cannot arrive. `src/tests/integration/billing-posting.db.spec.ts` tests split invoice, partial patient/insurer receipts, overpayment credit, manual refund allocation, concurrent retry, source ordering, parent reversal race, authorization/isolation, closed periods, bad accounts and injected-audit-failure rollback. `src/tests/vitest/billing-journal-ui.spec.tsx` covers lost-response reload/status and malformed browser state. The synthetic browser also posted an invoice and receipt and recovered a deliberately dropped response without a second journal.

### What is **not** completed

1. Source ordering currently uses transaction `created_at`; a transaction that began before waiting for an invoice lock can appear earlier than its true serialized business event. Reversed or untracked predecessors may block subsequent postings. A reliable ordering model and an authorized, auditable **corrected repost** workflow are still needed; the immutable unique source link does not by itself provide one. Do not bypass these guards or manually edit journals.
2. The bridge has no dedicated source posting for insurance claim recognition/adjudication/remittance, credit notes, invoice adjustments or a complete compensating refund correction. An explicitly attributed insurer receipt can be posted as a receipt, but that is **not** a complete insurance claim/remittance accounting workflow. Generic manual journals are not a substitute for a source-linked, reconciled implementation.
3. `/api/accounting/billing-consistency` and `/api/accounting/billing-ledger-review` inspect selected invoice, receipt and refund sources, identify missing/multiple/mismatched source journals and compare amounts/lines; the invoice selection period is based on invoice creation date. `/api/accounting/billing-journal-exceptions` is a paginated journal-side complement for known billing source types and selected journal dates. Read every page. The reports are snapshots, not an automatic repair or an exhaustive historical, claim, remittance, bank or manual-journal reconciliation. An amount match does not validate account classification, actual cash movement or payer settlement.
4. Refund creation has scoped permissions, receipt reference, immutable records, locking, over-refund checks, audit and idempotent recovery, but **no independent approval/separation-of-duties step**. Dedicated attribution/refund and multi-path race review, compensating correction and safe external-provider refund integration remain. No provider transfer is initiated by the manual refund record.

## Facility tariff report and intake

No real facility tariff report has been generated: there was no authorized facility dataset or approved schedule. The implementation can produce a scoped, read-only paginated report identifying unmapped scenarios, duplicate codes, invalid/inactive/expired tariffs, missing/non-positive prices, incompatible/conflicting links, and aggregated billed codes or references missing from the current catalogue. A `RESOLVED` result is not price approval and free-text procedures and inventory proxies are not an exhaustive service catalogue.

The authorized JSON batch API validates 1–200 explicit positive prices, explicit `autoBill`, codes and enabled modules, then atomically creates new tariffs and audit/batch identities after an operator attests a documented approval reference. It neither verifies that approval nor overwrites existing tariff rows. Same-key concurrent retries, conflict, facility/permission denial and audit-failure rollback have synthetic PostgreSQL HTTP tests. The workflow, endpoints, pagination and retry caveats are in [`docs/billing/facility-tariff-import.md`](../billing/facility-tariff-import.md). The blank CSV is an intake worksheet, not an importer. The facility must supply and approve all actual prices, mappings and clinical units.

## Claims, patient evidence and integration readiness

A finalized invoice retains a facility/patient and responsibility snapshot; patients and historical invoices are not replaced. Receipt attribution is explicit and never inferred from payment method. SHA and private claim preparation does not automatically pay or mutate an invoice, but **still permits unfinalized invoices** and therefore does not yet enforce the finalized snapshot as the sole claim basis. Evidence preview requires clinical and billing authority, uses selected same-patient/encounter records and records access; referral relevance requires explicit selection. It is not a payer transmission. Additional malformed/cross-encounter, admission and attribution/refund race tests are needed.

Scoped SHA claim summaries/details, visit authorizations and preauthorization responses now withhold stored consent tokens and FHIR bundles; claim responses also omit raw adjudication JSON, submission error arrays and status metadata. Preauthorization reads require both clinical and billing permission and are access-audited. A complete review of every provider-derived free-text error/status and every other insurance endpoint is still required before real transport is enabled; do not treat these targeted redactions as a blanket privacy certification.

Durable dispatch guards prevent concurrent SHA/private submissions or conflicting claim mutations while an outcome is unresolved. **There is no authorized recovery/release workflow for a crashed or ambiguous dispatch reservation.** Do not blindly retry or clear guards. Eligibility, benefit estimates, preauthorization, claim acknowledgement/adjudication and actual payment are different states. A provider HTTP 200 or request acknowledgement is not payment; manual local data and mocked responses are not insurer confirmation.

### Migration history transparency

Draft migrations 0037–0040 were revised after being applied to earlier isolated synthetic databases. Those old synthetic databases were not reset and their recorded hashes no longer match the final drafts. Fresh v2–v5 synthetic databases applied the revised chain; that does not repair older databases. Migrations 0041/0042 were subsequently applied to fresh synthetic databases and must not be edited after application. For any database with drift, stop, inspect schema and migration hashes, make a verified backup and devise an authorized **additive** repair; do not fake journal state, edit applied migrations or silently discard records. Migration 0033 may reject historical duplicate accessions, including legitimate shared specimens. A lab-led review and explicit migration plan are mandatory.

## Controlled browser-access and deployment procedure

1. Use a separate approved non-production environment with synthetic patients and transactions. Independently verify the database is new/disposable before exercising the full historical chain. For an existing facility database, do **not** apply the synthetic fresh-chain procedure: identify its exact migration history and conflicts, take and restore-test an authorized backup, review 0002/0033/0041 and all pending SQL, and rehearse a signed migration/rollback plan on an authorized isolated copy. Never reset or alter the migration journal to get past an error.
2. Follow the [deployment guide](../deployment/truehost-cpanel.md) and root README. Use the locked dependencies (`npm ci`), `npm run format:check`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, a clean-checkout `npm test` with a disposable PostgreSQL DB, then `npm run build:deploy` and `npm run verify:deploy`. `db:preflight`, `db:migrate:dry` and `db:status:all` are inspection tools; writes such as `db:migrate` or permission seeding require explicit operational approval and a reviewed plan. The application does not migrate automatically on startup.
3. Provide SQL settings, a stable `FIELD_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, session/proxy/HTTPS settings and other secrets through the protected host environment or a secret manager; never commit, print or paste credentials into tickets or logs. Preserve the encryption key and backup/restore strategy securely. Use the existing `src/bootstrap-admin.ts` with operator-supplied `ADMIN_USERNAME`, `ADMIN_EMAIL` and a strong `ADMIN_PASSWORD` in that protected environment. It hashes the password and does not overwrite an existing account's password. Provision facility users with explicitly scoped least-privilege roles; the platform bootstrap account is not a facility finance account. No reusable browser-test credentials are included here.
4. Run the server from `build/server.js` behind the approved HTTPS reverse proxy and appropriate origin/trust-proxy configuration. Keep production integrations disabled. With explicitly authorized synthetic test data, execute the [synthetic smoke procedure](../testing/SYNTHETIC_BROWSER_SMOKE.md), then have facility staff perform documented role-specific browser acceptance and retain non-sensitive evidence. The automated smoke generates disposable credentials and does not make a human-usable acceptance account; the facility operator must provision those accounts securely.
5. Before any real use, close the engineering blockers above, review the chart of accounts, pricing, authorization separation, security, privacy, clinical workflows and backup/restore, obtain facility and provider approvals, and test every enabled integration with officially supplied credentials in an authorized environment. No real deployment, migration, clinical sign-off or provider approval is represented as completed.
