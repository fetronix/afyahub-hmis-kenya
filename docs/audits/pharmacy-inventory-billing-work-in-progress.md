# Pharmacy inventory billing — work in progress

**Status: not release-ready.** This records synthetic test evidence, not clinical, financial, facility, payer, or production approval. The user-required medicine return/dispensing reversal workflow is not implemented. Existing untracked stock also lacks a safe reconciliation workflow. Do not deploy this as a completed pharmacy billing change.

## What is implemented

- Prescription creation does not deduct stock or add an invoice item. An actual authorized dispense locks the facility inventory item and its batches, checks the prescribed remaining quantity, selects unexpired lots in FEFO order using the facility/tenant timezone, and records the dispensation, per-lot allocations, stock deduction, retry identity, audit and durable billing work together. The expiry date is the last usable calendar day. A missing or invalid facility timezone blocks a new dispensing; a retry of an already recorded dispensing uses the original evidence.
- New dispensing is blocked when no batch/expiry evidence exists. A recorded plain stock count is not proof that medicine is in date. Where batch quantities cannot cover the request, the transaction is rejected. `inventory_batches` allocations plus the immutable dispensation form the stock-movement evidence; this change does not introduce a second stock ledger.
- The selected, active facility inventory item's positive `selling_price` is captured while the item is locked. A zero, missing, or invalid price is not a free service: the clinical dispensing is recorded, and its billing attempt remains unresolved for review. A later price cannot reprice it. Unlinked or legacy dispensing without complete, immutable prescription/price/batch evidence also remains unresolved.
- For evidenced pharmacy dispensing, automatic billing bypasses medicine and generic pharmacy service tariffs. It uses the existing billing engine and invoice, with the actual supplied quantity, the snapshot price and total, facility/tenant/patient, prescription, dispensing ID, selected inventory item, product description/unit and batch allocations. Partial fills get separate event-time prices; stable retry keys and the existing unique invoice-item identity prevent duplicate stock deductions and invoice lines. Non-pharmacy facility service/tariff billing is retained.
- An authorized user with both `inventory.manage` and `billing.update` can change a positive selling price with a reason and expected `price_revision`; the item lock, change and audit are transactional. A stale revision or concurrent competing change is rejected. Initial positive item pricing also needs the financial permission and has a transactional creation audit. The global SUPER_ADMIN boundary does not confer facility finance authority.
- Inventory-priced items can be finalized, paid and included in existing revenue reports without fabricating a tariff ID. Ledger journals are **not** posted automatically; the existing reviewed, source-linked bridge remains separate. Local SHA and private-claim preparation leaves such medicine lines unmapped rather than inferring an unrelated payer tariff by description. This is not evidence of payer submission, validation, approval, payment or live connectivity.

## Schema and historical preservation

Migrations `0060` and `0061` are additive: they add dispensing snapshots, billing-line inventory/prescription/batch provenance, scoped `NOT VALID` foreign keys, evidence immutability guards and an inventory price revision. They do not backfill historical dispense prices or alter historical prices, invoices, payments, journals, stock counts or audits. Legacy NULL snapshots are deliberately not automatically backfilled. The `NOT VALID` constraints protect new or modified writes but do **not** certify all old records are valid; investigate historical exceptions separately.

The migrations were applied only to isolated synthetic databases. An upgrade exercise created legacy synthetic inventory, dispense, invoice, line, charge-attempt and audit rows under schema 0059; after applying 0060–0061 those six row sets compared unchanged (excluding newly added metadata), new dispensing/billing evidence remained NULL, the original selling price stayed `24.75`, and an attempted silent snapshot backfill failed with PostgreSQL `23514`. `price_revision` initializes to 1 without changing the price.

For any future deployment, first obtain appropriate authorization and backups, review the migration plan and database-specific integrity/privilege impacts, and ensure migrations through 0061 are applied before dependent application code. Migration 0060 was revised during development to conform to the repository's guarded-DDL checks after earlier *synthetic-only* applications; fresh synthetic databases were used for the final tests. Earlier disposable synthetic databases report a checksum difference and must not be treated as deployment evidence. Do not edit applied production migration history or drop snapshots on rollback; a rollback must preserve and account for any new financial/clinical records.

## Outstanding blockers and operational limitations

1. **Medicine returns and dispensing reversals are not supported.** There is no integrated, authorized, idempotent stock return/credit-note workflow tied to invoices, payments, claims and already-posted journals. The pharmacy screen warns of this. Do not delete or edit original dispensing/invoice records, silently restore stock, create negative invoice lines, or treat a refund as a medicine return. Existing finalization rejects negative lines and SHA assembly clamps quantities, so that shortcut is unsafe. A controlled, reviewed design and tests for partial/full returns, paid/finalized/posted invoices, closed periods, payer allocation and replay are still required.
2. **Untracked stock cannot currently be reconciled through a safe supported workflow.** It is blocked from new dispensing; receiving a new batch increases stock and must not be used as a conversion of existing stock. A verified, audited physical reconciliation process and usability review are needed before rollout. This preserves the old data but changes operational availability.
3. The server's dispensing and batch status calculations use the configured facility/tenant timezone. The existing client-side expiry countdown and opening-stock pre-check use the browser's local timezone, so the display or preliminary refusal can disagree near midnight if the browser and facility zones differ. The server remains authoritative; the UI requires a facility-zone correction before release.
4. Price configuration is a current positive inventory price and item-active state; no scheduled/effective-dated medicine price or authorized free-medicine workflow is added. Legacy unpriced dispensations need a separately approved evidence-based correction process. No product-substitution or prescription identity is inferred from text.
5. Payer mappings for inventory-priced medicine remain explicitly unresolved, not inferred; payer-specific mapping, contract validation, integration and end-to-end settlement need their own reviewed workflow. No real payer or production system was contacted.
6. The full suite remains red as described below, and facility UAT, role/security review, real-data readiness analysis and backup/rollback review are still required.

## Test evidence

All database and HTTP fixtures below were synthetic on isolated loopback PostgreSQL, not production:

- Fresh install: all **62 migrations** through 0061 applied, and `db:status:all` reported ready for the synthetic database.
- New pharmacy DB/HTTP acceptance: **19 passed**. Covers no prescription charge, actual FEFO product/price/quantity, partial fills and historic prices, blocked excess/inactive/expired/untracked stock, idempotent parallel retries, facility and tenant isolation, timezone snapshots/replay, batch expiry mismatch, unknown price review, crash recovery, financial authorization/audit including SUPER_ADMIN, optimistic/concurrent price updates, finalization/payment/revenue, separate balanced idempotent ledger posting, unmapped local SHA/private claims and immutable legacy/new evidence.
- Final combined focused pharmacy, clinical, laboratory, concurrency, finalization, UI, batch, migration and local claims run: **173 passed across 15 files**. The inventory price UI suite verifies permission gating, submitted revision and reason, and stale-write feedback.
- Final full DB-required run on a fresh synthetic database: **200 files passed, 4 failed; 1,862 tests passed, 18 failed, 11 skipped**. The 18 failing test names and one setup-failing legacy catalogue suite match the previously documented unmodified baseline; one obsolete master-tariff pharmacy test now reaches the new missing-batch guard rather than its old assertion. The clinical-catalogue setup still calls a retired master endpoint. These failures/skips are not hidden or treated as a passing release gate.
- Typecheck: passed. Lint: **0 errors, 80 warnings**. Deployment build: passed with the existing large-chunk warning. Synthetic claims and ledger tests establish local behavior only.

No merge, deployment, production migration, real patient data, or external insurer transaction is implied by these results.
